Our Sponsors

Why should you sponsor us?

Our mission is to provide high school students, regardless of their prior experience, with the opportunity to delve into the world of robotics. As they progress through our program, they develop the skills and mindset to confidently address complex technical challenges and craft innovative solutions in fast-paced environments. Since FRC is an expensive program, we rely on sponsorships, grants, and donations to cover robot materials, registration, tools, and outreach.

Every gift counts: our five sponsorship levels range from a signed team photo to your logo on our robot and team shirts. See the sponsorship levels in our business plan. Donations are tax-deductible as allowed by law.
