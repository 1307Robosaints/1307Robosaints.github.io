2026 Business Plan · Team 1307 Robosaints · St. Thomas Aquinas High School, Dover, NH

Mission Statement

At Saint Thomas Aquinas High School Robotics, our mission is to inspire innovation and leadership through hands-on experience in STEM. By supporting us, sponsors invest in the future engineers and leaders who will shape tomorrow's world. Together, we build more than robots—we build a brighter future.

Who Are We?

The Robosaints design and build robots in the St. Thomas Aquinas makerspace, with students leading the charge under the guidance of teachers and parent mentors. This support has allowed us to expand our outreach, grow our team, and positively impact our community. For instance, we've launched a LEGO robotics program at a local elementary school, teaching 5th–8th graders the basics of coding and engineering. We also visit area schools to demonstrate our robots, offering younger students hands-on experience.

Founded in 2004, the Robosaints serve as an extracurricular program for students in grades 9–12, providing opportunities to develop skills in engineering, programming, critical thinking, leadership, and teamwork. Our goal is to apply academic knowledge to solve real-world technical challenges while also focusing on marketing, finance, business, and communication—key facets of any successful enterprise.

Despite having fewer members than many other teams, our robots are robust and competitive, a testament to our dedication and resourcefulness. In FIRST competitions, our ability to build cost-effective yet capable robots sets us apart, proving that passion and ingenuity can rival even the largest teams.

What is FIRST?

FIRST (For the Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is an organization that runs robotics competitions for students ages five to eighteen. FIRST prepares them for the future through Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). The FIRST Robotics Competition covers a league of high school students in which “under strict rules, limited resources, and the guidance of volunteer mentors, including engineers, teachers, business professionals, parents, alumni and more, teams of students build and program robots to perform challenging tasks against a field of competitors. They must also raise funds, design a team ‘brand,’ hone teamwork skills, and perform community outreach.” (firstinspires.org)

The FIRST Robotics Competition season consists of a six-week build phase to accomplish conceptualization, design, fabrication, and testing of a robot that accomplishes a series of competitive sport-like tasks for points. The robot is operated autonomously through its internal programming and through radio control.

Our Growth Over the Last Few Years

  • Upgraded from a converted storage closet to a dedicated robotics makerspace, featuring an adjoining woodshop and additional classroom space to integrate with the St. Thomas Aquinas science curriculum.
  • Enhanced our facilities with 3D printers, a metal lathe, and a CNC machine to support robotics and physics programs.
  • Introduced robotics school spirit wear, including custom buttons and team logo shirts for competitions.
  • Established a leadership committee and formalized team bylaws.
  • Expanded team membership from 5 to 16 members over the past two years.
  • Guided other students in completing 3D printing projects that support academic initiatives in the arts and sciences.
  • Launched an elective week-long “winter term” program, offering new students an introduction to the world of robotics.

Future Plans

  • Strengthen recruitment across all areas.
  • Enhance connections with alumni.
  • Refurbish old robots to optimize them for demonstrations and provide hands-on learning for new recruits.
  • Promote FIRST in the Seacoast area by increasing membership and supporting other robotics leagues.
  • Continue to support local FIRST LEGO League teams.
  • Forge stronger connections with the local business community.

Organizational Structure

The Robosaints team is composed of students specializing in Engineering, Computer Programming, and Business. Working collaboratively under the guidance of our mentors, team members focus on productivity, progress, and effective communication. Student team leaders, each overseeing engineering design, programming, and business analysis, report directly to the Robosaints Student President or Vice President.

The Engineering Design leader manages electrical, mechanical, and Computer-Aided Design (CAD) tasks. The Programming leader handles all robot control functions, including pre-programmed operations. The Business leader develops our business plan, manages external relations, formulates strategy, and monitors the budget.

Team Expenditures

Team expenditures are an important part of managing a high school FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team, as they ensure that the team has the resources necessary to design, build, and compete with a successful robot. Major expenses include robot materials and components, such as motors, electronics, raw materials, tools, and mechanical parts, as well as registration fees for the year and competitions. Additional costs may include team apparel, safety equipment, software, and outreach activities within the community. Since FRC is an expensive program, we rely on sponsorships, grants, and donations to cover these expenses. Careful budgeting and responsible spending help ensure that funds are used effectively, allowing all team members to participate and giving the team the best possible opportunity to succeed.
