Logos

The mark is a gear crossed with a scope reticle, with a radar sweep in the corner the tile cuts away. Use the main logo first; the others are for spaces it doesn't fit. Use the white versions on navy, black or photos, and the one-color versions for vinyl, engraving and embroidery.

Using the logo

Leave clear space on every side at least as big as the tile's cut corner (c). Smallest sizes: the mark 16 px on screen or ¼ in in print; the main logo 120 px or 1 in wide.

  • Write the name as one word with "scout" in lowercase: 1307scout.
  • Use the white version on navy, black or dark photos; put the logo on a navy or white plate over busy photos.
  • Use the one-color files when a printer allows only one ink.
  • Don't stretch, squash, rotate or tilt it.
  • Don't recolor parts of it, add outlines, shadows or glows, or move the pieces around.
  • Don't retype the wordmark in another font: use the files.
  • Don't mix it with the Robosaints team logo into one new logo. They can sit side by side.

Stickers

Ready for a sticker printer: send the SVG. The die-cut sticker is cut along its white plate; the round and hex ones along their white rims.
