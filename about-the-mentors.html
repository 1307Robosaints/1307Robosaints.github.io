Mr. Chrystoph Demos

Mr. Chrys "Kit" Demos is the chief mentor for the Robosaints 1307 FRC robotics team. He teaches physics and introductory electronics at St. Thomas Aquinas High School. He brings a ten-year background in research/engineering in advanced reentry materials and RF technology with a Small Business Innovative Research (SBIR) company for the Department of Defense and industrial engineering.

Mr. Demos has taught science for over twenty years. He joined St. Thomas Aquinas High School in 2019. Outside of teaching, he performs as a jazz bassist.
