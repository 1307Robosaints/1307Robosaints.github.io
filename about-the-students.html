About Us

FRC team 1307, also known as The Robosaints, is a competitive high school robotics team. Each year, we get a new challenge and work together to build an innovative robot that can compete at the highest levels. The team has three main departments: Mechanical, Software, and Business. Each department is overseen by a student department leader, while the team president provides overall guidance and direction. New students are always welcome to join our team and are encouraged to move around between departments to gain a well-rounded experience, and to find what they are good at and enjoy most. As juniors and seniors, students have the chance to run for leadership positions within the team. While the team is provided funding for competition registration by St. Thomas Aquinas High School, all of the costs associated with building and competing are covered by the team's fundraising efforts. Last season our business department raised over $14,000, a record for the team!
