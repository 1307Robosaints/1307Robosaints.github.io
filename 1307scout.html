1307scout

The scouting platform for competitive robotics. Collect robot data in the pits, collect real-time match data, and share exactly what other teams need to see.

Sign in to get started

Built for competition day

1307scout is designed around how scouting actually happens — fast data entry between matches, instant team lookup at the strategy table, and controlled sharing with alliance partners.

Configurable match forms

Staff define the fields — auto points, climb level, notes, whatever the season needs. The form updates immediately for every scout on the team.

Works offline

Entries saved when the venue Wi-Fi drops. Drafts are stored on the device and submitted automatically when the connection returns.

Official results, synced

Link an event to its FRC or TBA code and 1307scout pulls official match scores automatically — no re-typing results by hand. On competition day, staff can set the current event to refresh every 2 minutes for the day, and anyone on the team can press Resync data to pull the latest results right away. Scouting entries are checked against those results, and any match that needs a second look is flagged.

Scoped share codes

Generate a QR code scoped to one team, one event, or both. Set an expiry, hand it to an alliance partner, and revoke it when the match is over.

Three levels of access

Everyone sees exactly what they need — nothing more.

Staff

Set up events and form fields, manage share codes, enter playoff alliances, see all scouting data across the team, and review an audit log of who changed what. Access is granted by allowlist.

Team members

Sign in with an account attached to your school's domain to enter match data and look up any team's history, robot parameters, official results, and letter-grade rating.

Guests

Alliance partners and other teams use a share code to view specific data — a single team's record, or a whole event — without seeing anything else.

Ready to scout?

Team members sign in with their school Google account. Everyone else creates a free account or uses a share code.
