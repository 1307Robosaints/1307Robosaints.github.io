1307scout

Free scouting for every FRC team. Scout matches and pits, look up any team's history and official results, plan each match with your alliance, and run your pit, from one app that keeps working when the venue Wi-Fi doesn't.

  • Every FRC team, worldwide
  • Free
  • Official results synced
  • Scouts offline
Sign in or create an account Get the app

Built for competition day

1307scout is built around how scouting really happens: fast entry in the stands, quick lookups at the strategy table, a pit that knows when the next match is, and an alliance selection list that's ready before the call.

01

Scout

Collect good data, even with no signal

Match and pit scouting

One form for every match and one pit entry per team per event, with fields set for each season's game: counts, choices, notes, and robot photos straight from the camera.

Works offline

Match entries made with no connection are kept on the device and sent when it reconnects, or handed to a scout who is online by QR code. Install the app and it opens with no connection at all.

Scout shifts

Give each scout a driver-station seat for a range of matches, one at a time or as a rotation. The Scout page shows each scout their next match and team, ready to fill in.

Match flags

Every played match is checked against the schedule, official per-robot results, alliance scores, other scouts and the team's usual numbers. Anything that looks wrong or missing is flagged, with the robots to scout next.

02

Know every team

Scouting data shared by every team, beside the official record

Team Database

Any team's scouted and official match history, pit data and photos, score breakdown, OPR for every scoring category, trend charts and every scouted note. Event rankings, CSV export, and a copy you can download for offline.

Official results, synced

Scores come from FRC Events and The Blue Alliance on their own: every event that's under way refreshes about every 2 minutes while matches run, and Resync data pulls the latest right away. Your team's pages open on the event you're registered for.

Ratings you can check

Two separate letter-grade ratings, one from scouting and one from official results, plus alliance grades and win chance. Every formula is explained on How Ratings Work, along with how past predictions held up.

Alliance View

Six robots side by side on one screen, red against blue, with each alliance's grade and estimated win chance. Every match listing opens here in one click.

03

Compete

From the pit to the field

Event Viewer

For a laptop or TV in the pit: an event's livestream beside the match on the field, your next match, your record, and a quick alliance comparison for any match, with its match video once it's posted and a link to share it. The bracket appears in the playoffs, and it all keeps itself up to date.

Our Matches and alerts

Every match your team has at the event, with partners, opponents and win chance. Turn on match alerts and your phone tells you shortly before you need to queue, live from FRC Nexus at events that use it.

Match Strategy

A to-scale field map with each match's robots in their stations. Drag robots to their starting spots and draw the plan. Alliance partners on 1307scout share one plan the other alliance can't see, saved for review after the match.

Pit Crew

Your robot's pre-match checklist, batteries and their voltages, repairs and notes, with one line that says whether the next match is ready to go.

04

Playoffs and sharing

Alliance selection, the bracket, and controlled sharing

Pick List

Your team's ranked list for alliance selection. Drag to reorder, tier each team (first pick, second pick, maybe, do not pick) and add notes. Teams grey out as they're picked.

Playoff Selection

Alliances update on every screen as they form, then switch to the official ones once they're published. Every team still available is ranked beside them, with your pick list's next choice named.

Playoff Bracket

The double-elimination bracket, filled from the alliances and moved along by official results as the event syncs.

Share codes

Give an alliance partner, a parent or a team waiting to be verified a link or QR code for chosen teams or events. Codes expire within 90 days and can be revoked any time. Your team's own plans are never shared.

Who sees what

Everyone sees exactly what they need, and the database enforces it, not just the page.

  • Shared by every teamScouting entries, pit data, official results, match flags and playoff alliances. Every verified team scouts into the same pool.
  • Your team's ownStrategy plans, the pick list, the pit crew log and scout shifts. Only your team's members see them.
Team members

Tell us your team when you sign up. You're verified right away by your school email if your team listed its domain, or by an invitation, join code or invite link; otherwise you're a guest until one of its managers verifies you. Members scout, look up any team, use the team's tools, and make share codes.

Team managers

Run their team on Team Management: verify people waiting, invite by email or link, list the email domain that verifies members at once, make other members managers, hand out scout shifts, choose whether members or only managers can change strategy plans, the pick list, the pit crew log, scout shifts and the default event, or make share codes, and set the pit crew checklist. Site staff confirm a team's first manager with the team.

Site staff

Run the site: events and each season's scouting form, playoff alliances and bracket tiebreakers, approving each team's first manager, and an audit log of who changed what.

Guests

Anyone not on a verified team (yet) can use a share code from a team member to see what it covers, such as one team's record or a whole event, and nothing else.

Get the 1307scout app

Prefer an app to a browser tab? 1307scout installs on phones, tablets, Chromebooks, and laptops straight from this site. No app store, nothing extra to download, and it's the same 1307scout with the same features and the same account.

  • Opens from your home screen, dock, or Start menu in its own window.
  • Opens with no connection. Match entries made offline are kept on the device and sent when you reconnect.
  • Match alerts arrive like any app's notifications.
  • Always up to date. Every update to the website is in the app the next time it opens online.
