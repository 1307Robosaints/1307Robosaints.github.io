Share Data

Every team on 1307scout already sees all the scouting data. A share code lets someone who isn't on a team here, such as an alliance partner, a parent or a team still waiting to be verified, see a slice of it: one or more teams, one or more events, or both. They sign in with a free account and type the code or scan its QR code. It works until it expires, you revoke it, or you leave your team.

Your team's managers have kept share codes to managers. Ask one of them for a code.
