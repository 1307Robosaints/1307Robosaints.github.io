Install steps and offline help

Match alerts

Get a notification on this device before a team needs to queue, on competition days while auto-sync is on. At events that run queuing on FRC Nexus, it comes when the match is queuing or about 10 minutes before its estimated queue time; elsewhere, when the team's next match is 3 or fewer matches away. Tapping it opens that match's strategy plan. Each device is turned on separately. On iPhone and iPad this works only in the installed app.
