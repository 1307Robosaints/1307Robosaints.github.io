Team Management

Your team on 1307scout: who's on it, who manages it, and how its own pages work.

Site staff: act as a team

Acting as a team makes you its manager on every page (strategy, pit crew, pick list, shifts and this one) until you stop. Everything you change is in the audit log.

Which FRC team are you on?

If your team listed your school's email domain, you're verified right away. Otherwise one of its managers, or site staff, verifies you, and until then you use 1307scout as a guest.

Join with a code

A code or invite link from one of your team's managers puts you on the team straight away.

Manage your team

For a team's lead mentor or student leader. Site staff check with your team before approving you, so tell them how to do that. If your team already has a manager on 1307scout, ask them instead: any manager can make you one.

Waiting to be verified

These accounts say they're on your team. Verify only people you know are; until then they're guests.

Invite by email

The person is on your team as soon as they sign in with that email and accept (1307scout doesn't send the email: tell them). Invitations last 30 days.

Invite links and join codes

Anyone who opens the link or enters the code joins your team as a member. Share it with your team only, and revoke it when you're done.

Verify by email domain

Someone who says they're on your team and signs in with an email at one of these domains is verified right away, such as your school's (yourschool.org). Free email providers like gmail.com can't be used.

Who can change what

Everyone on the team can see these pages. Managers can always change them; choose whether members can too.

Pit Crew setup

The pre-match checklist on Pit Crew, one item per line (up to 30). Checklists already saved keep the items they had.

Become a manager

Ask one of your team's managers listed above: any manager can make you one. If your team has no manager left, instead.

Leave the team

You'll keep your account and your scouting entries, but lose your team's pages until you join a team again.
